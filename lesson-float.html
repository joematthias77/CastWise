Float fishing explained

Float fishing lets you present bait at a chosen depth and watch the float for signs that a fish has taken it.

1. Set the depth

Use a plummet to find the bottom, then set the float so your bait sits at the depth you want. Fish may feed on the bottom or higher in the water.

2. Balance the float

Small shot help the float sit correctly. Follow the float maker’s guidance and keep shot secure on the line.

3. Feed little and often

Start with small amounts of suitable bait. Too much loose feed can fill fish up or draw them away from your hookbait.

4. Watch for bites

A float may dip, slide away or lift depending on the rig and species. Learn what normal movement looks like before striking.

5. Strike smoothly

When you see a clear bite, lift the rod with a controlled movement to take up slack and set the hook. Avoid a violent jerk with light tackle.

Remember: Keep the hook and shot sizes appropriate for the target fish and local rules.
