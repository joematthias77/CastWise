Pole fishing lesson

How pole fishing works

Pole fishing presents a short line accurately without a conventional reel. It can be a very effective way to fish lakes and canals.

1. Know the parts

A pole is made of sections. The top kit carries the rig and may contain elastic, which cushions runs from fish.

2. Choose the elastic

Elastic strength should match your target species, hooklength and venue. Ask a tackle shop or experienced pole angler to help you match the system.

3. Plumb the depth

Attach a plummet to the hook and lower it into the swim. Adjust the float until the bait is presented at the intended depth.

4. Ship out smoothly

Keep the pole balanced and move it forward in a controlled way. Avoid swinging the rig around other anglers or overhead branches.

5. Land and unhook carefully

Use the elastic and pole to guide the fish towards a landing net. Keep the net ready and handle the fish with wet hands.

Remember: Never lift a large fish directly out of the water using the pole alone.
