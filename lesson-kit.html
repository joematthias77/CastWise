Basics lesson

Your first fishing setup

You do not need loads of equipment to start fishing. Begin with a simple setup suited to the water and fish you want to catch.

1. Choose the right rod

A general-purpose float or feeder rod can be a good starting point for stillwaters and canals. Pick a rod designed for your method and venue, rather than simply buying the longest one.

2. Match your reel

A fixed-spool reel is common for beginners using a rod and reel. Choose one that balances comfortably with the rod and check that the drag works smoothly.

3. Pick suitable line

Line strength should match the target fish, tackle and likely snags. Check for nicks or fraying before each session and replace damaged line.

4. Pack essential tackle

Start with hooks, floats or feeders, shot, swivels, bait, a disgorger and a landing net. Add an unhooking mat when the species and venue require one.

5. Start with one method

Float fishing is a clear way to learn bite detection and depth. A simple feeder setup is another option when you want to present bait on the bottom.

Remember: Check venue rules, licence requirements and fish-care expectations before your first session.
