Bait lesson

Fishing bait made simple

You do not need a huge bait collection to begin. Start with one or two permitted options and learn how fish respond.

1. Maggots and worms

Common choices for many coarse fish. Keep them stored appropriately and use only where the venue allows them.

2. Sweetcorn and bread

These are easy to handle and can be useful for a range of species. Use small hookbaits and avoid feeding more than you need.

3. Pellets and boilies

Often used for carp and other larger fish. Check the fishery’s bait rules and use sizes that suit your hook and target.

4. Groundbait and loose feed

Small amounts can attract fish to an area, but too much feed may be wasteful or against venue rules. Start lightly and observe.

5. Match bait to method

A tiny hook and float may suit a maggot, while a feeder or carp rig may use a different bait. Make sure the hook point remains clear enough to work.

Remember: Always check bait restrictions, especially at stocked fisheries and waters with biosecurity rules.
