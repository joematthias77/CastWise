Lure fishing lesson

Choosing your first lures

Lure fishing uses an artificial bait that you retrieve to attract predatory fish such as perch and pike.

1. Start with simple lures

Small soft plastics, spinners and shallow-running hard lures are useful starter options. Choose a lure weight within your rod’s casting rating.

2. Match lure to water

In clear water, natural colours can be useful. In coloured water or low light, a more visible lure may help. Let the fish and conditions guide you.

3. Retrieve steadily

Cast, allow the lure to reach the desired depth and retrieve it with a consistent movement. Try small changes in speed if fish are not responding.

4. Protect yourself and fish

Check behind you before casting and keep hooks away from fingers. If targeting pike, use a suitable wire or bite-proof trace and proper long-nosed tools.

5. Cover water thoughtfully

Fan casts across different angles and depths rather than repeatedly casting to the same spot. Move if safe and permitted when an area looks inactive.

Remember: Follow local lure restrictions and use appropriate tools for safe unhooking.
