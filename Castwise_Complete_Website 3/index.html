Starter checklist

Simple float rig

  • Suitable rod and reel or pole
  • Main line and a correctly balanced float
  • Small shot to balance the float
  • Hooklength, hook and suitable bait
  • Plummet to check depth; landing net and unhooking tools

Before using any rig, check the fishery rules, use appropriate tackle for the likely fish, and ensure the setup is safe if line breaks.

Illustrated lesson

A fishing rig is just how the bits connect.

A rig is the arrangement of line, hook, bait and any weights or floats. Once you can recognise the parts, diagrams become much easier to understand.

This simplified float setup is a learning illustration, not a universal rig. Adjust tackle to the venue, target species and local rules.

1. FloatHelps suspend bait and shows bites.
2. ShotSmall weights help the float sit correctly.
3. Hook & baitPresented at the depth where fish may feed.
