# Castwise Complete Website

## How to use
1. Keep every file in this folder together.
2. Open `index.html` in a browser to preview the site.
3. The Knowledge Library cards open separate lesson pages.
4. The video tabs switch between Casting, Striking, and Landing.
5. The Fishing Knot Lab has three beginner knots with step-by-step illustrations.
6. The Castwise Toolkit includes a UK venue starter directory, tackle builder, quizzes and bite scenarios, interactive rig builder, fishing diary, fish guide, session planner, and local community demo.

## Important limitations in this no-backend version
- Diary entries, saved plans, profile details, and community posts use browser local storage on this device only. They do not create secure accounts, sync between devices, or publish posts to other users. Real accounts, syncing and a shared community require a backend and privacy/security setup.
- The venue directory is a starter list, not a verified live database. Always confirm fishing rights, permits, prices, opening times, current species information and local rules with the venue or controlling authority.
- Tackle recommendations use product categories and search links, not live inventory, checked prices or affiliate tracking. Specific product feeds, current prices and affiliate revenue require approved merchant/affiliate integrations.
- Weather data is not live; the planner gives a checklist and general seasonal/safety prompts. Live forecasts require a weather data service.
- The embedded instructional videos are hosted by their original publishers on YouTube and require an internet connection.
- Fish-identification entries and rig diagrams are educational aids, not definitive identification or universal rig instructions. Practise knots, test tackle, and follow venue rules and fish-care guidance.
- The newsletter form remains a demo and does not collect or send email addresses until connected to an email service.
