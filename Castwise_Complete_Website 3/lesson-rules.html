Pre-session checklist

Before you go fishing

A quick check before leaving home can prevent a wasted trip and helps protect you, other people and the fish.

1. Get permission

A rod licence does not give permission to fish anywhere. Check that the venue allows fishing and that you have the right ticket or club access.

2. Check the licence rules

In England and Wales, some freshwater fishing requires a rod fishing licence. Check the current official rules for your age, species and method.

3. Read venue rules

Check permitted methods, bait restrictions, close seasons, opening times, keepnets and fish-care requirements.

4. Check weather and access

Look at wind, rain, river levels, tides if relevant, and whether paths or banks are safe. Tell someone where you are going if fishing alone.

5. Pack fish-care kit

Take a landing net, suitable unhooking tools and an unhooking mat where appropriate. Bring water, warm or waterproof clothing and a bag for litter.

6. Leave the swim as you found it

Take all rubbish and line home. Avoid moving fish or water between venues and follow local biosecurity guidance.

Remember: Use the official GOV.UK rod fishing licence guidance and the specific venue’s own rules before every trip.
