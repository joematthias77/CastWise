Carp fishing: the basics

Carp fishing is about finding likely feeding areas, using suitable bait and tackle, and keeping fish safe throughout the session.

1. Choose a suitable venue

A well-managed day-ticket fishery can be a friendly place to learn. Check its rules before you go and ask which methods and baits are allowed.

2. Understand carp bait

Boilies, pellets, sweetcorn and other baits may work. Start with one suitable bait and avoid feeding excessive quantities. Follow the venue’s bait rules.

3. Use safe tackle

Choose a rod, reel, line and rig that suit the fish and venue. Use a fish-safe lead arrangement and never leave a fish tethered to unattended tackle.

4. Prepare for landing

Have a suitable landing net, an unhooking mat and appropriate unhooking tools ready before casting. Do not lift large carp by the line or stand them on dry ground.

5. Keep the fish calm

Wet your hands, support the fish properly and minimise time out of the water. Follow the fishery’s rules for photos and returning fish.

Remember: Ask an experienced angler or fishery staff to check your carp rig before using it.
