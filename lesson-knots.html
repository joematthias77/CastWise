Hooks, lines and knots

Hooks, line and knots are small parts of your setup, but they connect you to the fish. Choose them to suit your method and practise knots before fishing.

1. Match hook to bait and fish

A small hook is not automatically better. Choose a hook pattern and size that securely holds your bait and is appropriate for the fish you expect.

2. Check your line

Run the line through your fingers carefully to find rough or damaged sections. Replace line that is frayed, kinked or weakened.

3. Learn three beginner knots

Use the interactive knot lab on the home page to practise the Palomar, improved clinch and uni knots. Not every knot suits every line type, so check the line manufacturer’s advice.

4. Tighten and test

Moisten knots before tightening where appropriate, pull them down evenly, trim tag ends neatly and test with a firm pull before casting.

5. Stay hook-safe

Keep hooks covered when moving, use forceps or a disgorger to remove them, and never leave hooks where people or wildlife can step on them.

Remember: A knot that looks tidy still needs to be tested. Retie after snags or any sign of damage.
